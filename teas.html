Teas I can sell (茶)

While joining me at the gung-fu ceremony, sometimes I get asked if I sell tea. While I am not a full-fledged tea shop, I have recently started storing some teas in small batches, to let my guests buy!

Recently, while traveling through Sri Lanka, I came across Sara Cylon Tea, a local, small tea-maker, who let me taste some handmade teas. I am presenting some of these teas here. If you are like me, and want to add some exotic teas to your gubng-fu experience, you will love these teas!

Some of these teas were made to order, just in the last couple of months, to ensure that we get the freshest taste straight from the Central Highlands of Sri Lanka!
