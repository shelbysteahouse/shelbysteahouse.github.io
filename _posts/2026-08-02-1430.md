  We can reduce this by finding 132 (remainder 7) = 6 shifts
  <br/><br/>
  January adds 3 shifts;
  <br/><br/>
  February, no shifts;
  <br/><br/>
  And March, 3 shifts.
  <br/><br/>
  Finally 12 shifts for Apr 12th. Altogether, 6 + 3 + 3 + 12 = 24 shifts.
  <br/><br/>
  But we reduce this again as 24 (remainder 7) = 3 shifts.
  <br/><br/>
  3 shifts from Monday (the end of 1900) was a Thursday!!
